Home

Jones Harvey is a multimedia artist located in amiskwaciy waskahikan. They work primarily in multimedia visual art, but also enjoy working with other mediums including textiles and digital. This webpage provides some examples of their work. Thank you for visiting!

Note: All dates given follow format dd-mm-yy. All photos are by Jones Harvey.
